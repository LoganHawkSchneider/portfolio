## Problem & Objective

The strut seems to have collapsed. It now has a clunking noise when driving and bobs the vehicle towards the back driver side.
The biggest issue so far was finding replacement parts, this car doesnt use the standard suspension system but instead uses an air ride suspension/shock/strut system for the rear tires. The only replacements i could find for this vehicle were coil struts/shocks but only for the front.

So I get the joy of trying to DIY convert the rear into a coil system and delete the air ride system and hopefully try to keep the feel, if not improve the feel from the air ride system. (Although it was not used in the first place as there was a leak in the line that was yet to be fixed)
